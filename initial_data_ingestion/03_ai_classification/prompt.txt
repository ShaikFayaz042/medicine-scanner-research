### BACKGROUND: THE MEDICINE SCANNER APP

The **Medicine Scanner App** is a consumer and healthcare tool designed for patients, pharmacists, and medical professionals. When a user scans a medicine (by brand name, active formulation/API, batch number, or manufacturer name) or searches for a drug in India, the app queries its backend database to provide instant safety alerts:

1. **Safety & Quality Status**: Is this specific medicine or batch flagged as **Not of Standard Quality (NSQ)**, **Spurious**, **Adulterated**, or **Misbranded** by CDSCO or State drug testing labs?
2. **Prohibition & Ban Alerts**: Is this drug formulation or Fixed Dose Combination (FDC) **banned, prohibited, or unapproved** under Section 26A or Section 10A of the Drugs & Cosmetics Act (e.g., 156 FDCs banned list, 14 FDCs list, 328 FDCs list, Nimesulide animal ban, Colistin ban)?
3. **Recalls & Theft Alerts**: Is there an active **voluntary/mandatory recall** (e.g., Digene Gel recall, Baxter Tisseel Lyo recall, Ozurdex recall) or a **transit theft alert** (e.g., Sanofi Insulin Glargine batch theft)?
4. **Prescribing & Packaging Advisories**: Are there active **Schedule H1 prescription rules**, **mandatory QR/barcode packaging mandates for top 300 medicine brands**, **excipient disclosure warnings**, or **black box / prescribing information warnings** (e.g., Doxycycline CNS side effects, Ranitidine NDMA impurity, Carbimazole agranulocytosis advisory)?

### STRICT CLASSIFICATION RULES

#### 1. `process_queue` (IMPORTANT)
**STRICT REQUIREMENT**: The document **MUST** explicitly contain a **specific drug name** (e.g., *Oxytocin, Paracetamol, Meropenem, Olaparib, Nimesulide, Ranitidine, Doxycycline, Carbimazole, Digene, Chlorpheniramine, Phenylephrine*), **active pharmaceutical ingredient (API)**, **specific vaccine**, **brand name**, OR a **specific manufacturer name** (e.g., *Abbott, Sanofi, Takeda, Baxter, Novo Nordisk, DePuy, Medtronic*).

**Include in `process_queue` if the document contains:**
- Monthly Drug Alert lists (CDSCO / State Lab NSQ tables containing specific batch numbers, drug names, and manufacturer names).
- Official Gazette notifications banning/prohibiting specific drugs or FDCs under Section 26A / Section 10A.
- Unapproved drug warnings (e.g., Meropenem + Disodium EDTA injection warning, unapproved Enclomiphene combinations).
- Voluntary or mandatory drug/device recall letters (e.g., Digene Gel recall, Tisseel Lyo recall).
- Safety advisories on specific drugs (e.g., Doxycycline CNS side effects, Ranitidine NDMA impurity, Carbimazole agranulocytosis, SGLT2 Inhibitor safety warning).
- Specific batch theft alerts (e.g., Lantus SoloStar Insulin Glargine theft).
- Schedule H1 / Schedule H2 inclusions for specific drugs (e.g., Oseltamivir, Zanamivir, Tapentadol, Pregabalin, Oxytocin).
- Mandatory Barcode/QR code packaging mandates on top 300 medicine brands (GSR 823E).
- National List of Essential Medicines (NLEM) tables listing specific drugs.
- Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) or Package Insert dosage updates for specific drugs (e.g., ZyCoV-D SmPC, Empagliflozin package insert update).

---

#### 2. `junk` (IRRELEVANT / ADMINISTRATIVE NOISE)
**Exclude from `process_queue` and classify as `junk` if the document DOES NOT list specific drug names or specific manufacturer safety alerts.**

**Include in `junk` if the document is:**
- Right to Information (RTI) office orders, CPIO/CAPIO officer appointments.
- Administrative IT portal circulars (SUGAM portal launching, NSWS single window system, e-SANCHIT, ICEGATE, E-Vartalap, Visitor Management System).
- Internal CDSCO staff rotational transfers, probation completion orders, or HR notices.
- Office setup circulars, Public Relations Office (PRO) schedules, or internal office orders.
- Meeting schedule notices, WebEx video-conference links, or stakeholder meeting cancellation notices.
- Fee payment instructions, Bharatkosh challan payment procedures, or fee schedule circulars.
- Cosmetics import registration certificate surrender letters or cosmetic brand registration cancellations (unless involving a toxic drug contamination).
- Authorized Ports of Entry notifications (sea ports, airports, inland container depots like Gangavaram, Mundra, Cochin, ICD Dhannad).
- Appointments/Notifications of Government Analysts (GA) or Medical Device Testing Officers (MDTO) at CDL/CDTL/NIB laboratories.
- Generic rule amendments (e.g., Jan Vishwas Act compounding penalty rules, CRO registration procedures, DTAB reconstitutions) that do NOT list specific drug names or manufacturers.

---

#### 3. `manual_review` (AMBIGUOUS / LEGAL / DRAFTS)
**Classify as `manual_review` if human verification is required to decide whether actionable drug data exists.**

**Include in `manual_review` if the document is:**
- Corrigendums modifying serial numbers or text without the full context of the original referenced document.
- General pharma manufacturer application procedural circulars (e.g., instructions on how pharma companies should file applications for Kokate committee rational FDCs).
- Court case hearing status notices, High Court / Supreme Court direction updates (e.g., SLP status on 294 FDCs or 344 FDCs without complete list of drugs in the document text).
- Blank reporting form templates (e.g., blank Medical Device Adverse Event reporting form, blank public hearing form).
- Draft guidelines or draft frameworks (e.g., Draft Good Clinical Practices, Draft Good Distribution Practices, Draft risk-based medical device classification lists) awaiting final gazette release.
